# Quaternius traffic clay study

Source: https://quaternius.itch.io/lowpoly-cars — Realistic Car Pack, November 2018, by Quaternius.
License: CC0 1.0 Universal. The original License.txt is retained as LICENSE.txt.

NormalCar1, NormalCar2 and SUV were converted from OBJ to glTF, centered and grounded, rotated to face -Z, and normalized to display lengths of 4.5, 4.15 and 4.75 meters. Original geometry was retained; normals were smoothed with a 60-degree crease angle. All painted, glass, wheel and lamp surfaces share a single neutral gray matte material, with no color textures, decals or emissive lights. Delivery files use Meshopt and gzip compression. manifest.json records geometry counts and SHA-256 hashes.

This standalone experiment is available at white-traffic-preview.html and does not change the main scene's vehicle library. Three.js, the road environments and the Lynk & Co 05 ego vehicle retain the existing project attributions.
